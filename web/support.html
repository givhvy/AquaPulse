Support

AquaPulse runs entirely on your iPhone. These notes cover the usual questions.

How to use it

  • Set a daily water goal and glass size on Home.
  • Tap the glow button or the glass grid to log drinks in order.
  • Add extra rituals from the Rituals tab. Swipe to delete a non-water ritual.
  • The week board only marks days you complete.

Reminders

Enable reminders from Home, Board, or iOS Settings → Notifications → AquaPulse. If you do not see alerts, check Focus modes and notification permission.

Sign in with Apple

Optional. Use it if you want the display name from Apple. Sign out or delete data from Profile. Apple does not provide a profile photo; pick one yourself.

Delete my data

Open Profile → Delete account and reset data. That clears logs, rituals, photo, and the local Apple identifier. You can also delete the app.
