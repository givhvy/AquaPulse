Log water. Keep the rituals that matter.

AquaPulse is a dark, quiet reminder app for hydration and daily consistency. Glasses and streaks live on your iPhone. Sign in with Apple is optional. Nothing is sent to our servers.

AquaPulse is a personal reminder. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment for dehydration or any condition.

What it does

  • Daily water goal and sequential glass check-in
  • Optional rituals (stretch, read, walk, and your own)
  • A week board that only fills when you actually check in
  • Local reminders, name, and photo under your control
