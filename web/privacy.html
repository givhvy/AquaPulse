Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

AquaPulse is made by Huy (hello@huy.global). This policy describes how the iOS app handles information.

Data stays on your iPhone

AquaPulse does not operate a cloud account or analytics backend. Display name, water logs, rituals, reminder settings, and an optional profile photo are stored in app storage on your device (UserDefaults and a local image file).

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, Apple may give the app a user identifier, and on first grant a name and email (or a private relay email). We store those values on the device only so the profile can stay signed in. We do not send them to our servers. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to Apple’s authentication service.

Photos

If you pick a profile photo, the system photo picker copies an image onto the device. We do not upload photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, AquaPulse schedules local hydration and ritual reminders. Those notifications are created on-device and can be turned off in iOS Settings.

Tracking and selling

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising SDKs.

Health

AquaPulse is not a medical device and does not access Apple Health. Logged water is a personal reminder, not a clinical measurement.

Delete your data

In the app: Profile → Delete account and reset data. That signs you out of Apple on this device, removes the local photo, and clears logs and rituals. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

Children

AquaPulse is not directed at children under 13. Do not enter another person’s data.

Contact

Privacy questions: hello@huy.global
