## Description

AquaPulse helps you log water and simple daily rituals on your iPhone.

Set a daily glass goal, check in glass by glass, and optionally add habits like stretch, read, or walk. Streaks and the week board only move when you actually complete a ritual. All of that stays on this device.

Sign in with Apple is optional. You can use AquaPulse without an account, change your name and photo, or delete everything from Profile.

AquaPulse is a personal reminder. It is not medical advice and it does not connect to Apple Health.

To review: complete the short onboarding (you can skip the name), log a few glasses from Home, add or delete a ritual, open Profile for Privacy / Terms / Support and Delete account.
